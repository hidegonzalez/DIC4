\section{Conclusion}\label{sec:conclusion}
This template example summarized the MobileNetV3 architecture search methodology~\cite{howard2019mobilenetv3}, which unifies platform-aware NAS, NetAdapt layer-wise channel refinement, Squeeze-and-Excitation blocks, and the \textit{h-swish} activation.
Published ImageNet results confirm that MobileNetV3-Large achieves 75.2\% Top-1 accuracy at 219M MAdds while MobileNetV3-Small achieves 67.4\% Top-1 accuracy at 66M MAdds, outperforming MobileNetV2 baselines in both accuracy and computational efficiency.
